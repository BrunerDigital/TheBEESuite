## Findings

- **VERIFIED code defect:** the tuition cron substituted the current week when a biweekly or four-week assignment lacked its start period, bypassing the helper's missing-anchor guard and making it due in consecutive weeks. The fix fails closed for that assignment and surfaces a configuration failure. Kokomo's two current biweekly assignments had anchors W40 and W41 in the Sep 29 snapshot; this defect was not proven to have caused Kokomo's reported amount issue.
- **VERIFIED code defect:** the cron selected active children at all non-closed schools, ignoring the school's `tuitionBillingEnabled` and explicit pause flags. The fix filters the initial school set, then locks the family billing account and school exclusively before rechecking the school inside the invoice transaction. It preserves main's child lock and fresh enrollment/assignment check. The account-to-school lock order matches invoice/payment writers and avoids shared-lock upgrades across concurrent same-school invoices. A school pause committed before the school lock prevents the invoice; a pause submitted after the lock waits for that transaction to finish.
- **VERIFIED code defect:** the successful family-balance webhook set PAID and the negative ledger entry while preserving `stripePaymentIntentStatus: processing`. The fix records `succeeded` on verified success, including the concurrent fallback path, without replaying a payment or changing historical records.
- **UNVERIFIED live incident:** Granbury's Sep 29 live connected account had active card/transfers, charges and payouts enabled, no due requirements, but its account response did not include an active US bank-account ACH capability. Kokomo and Centennial had active ACH. Confirm payment-method configuration and a safe authenticated checkout preflight before attributing every reported Granbury failure to this cause. No provider capability change is included.
- **UNVERIFIED live incident:** Centennial tuition editor freezing was not reproduced. Source has unbounded async UI transitions; browser reproduction with the affected director session and network trace is still required. Five local saved methods were bound to a prior Connect account, so saved-method reauthorization remains a separate exact-target path.
- **HELD operational decision:** Kokomo had two current biweekly billing assignments at weekly base rates 18,900 and 20,250 cents, anchors W41 and W40. Its UI intentionally multiplies a weekly base rate by two per biweekly invoice. Another current assignment had weekly billing cadence and a biweekly plan cadence; source and director-approved rate intent must be reconciled before changing a plan, amount, or already-created invoice. No amount change is included.
- **VERIFIED accounting projection (Sep 29):** Granbury 59, Kokomo 34 and Centennial 47 current billing accounts had zero confirmed latest running-ledger-to-stored-balance mismatches. Earlier apparent exceptions were zero-balance accounts without ledger entries. Granbury had 5 and Centennial 2 positive family balances without an open invoice; those need provenance review, not automatic correction. Agency balances must use canonical agency-ledger types and source in `parent-billing-visibility.ts`; SQL here was corrected accordingly.
- **REFRESHED accounting projection (Oct 6):** Granbury 63, Kokomo 31 and Centennial 48 current billing accounts still had zero confirmed latest running-ledger-to-stored-balance mismatches. Granbury had 6 and Centennial 2 positive family balances without an open invoice. Kokomo had 6 DRAFT processing payments; Centennial had 4. These are local read-only projections, not provider settlement confirmation.
- **UNVERIFIED current ACH:** Sep 29 local DRAFT processing payments were Kokomo 6 and Centennial 3; no processing payment in the sampled three schools had a local negative ledger debit. Provider status, webhook receipt, invoice and balance must be reconciled before retries or settlement claims. A Sep 30 read-only join found 44 pending Checkout completion receipts: 36 matched PAID local payments, 4 VOID, 2 FAILED returned, and 2 DRAFT processing at Holly Hill. Pending receipt status alone is not evidence of 44 unposted charges. Nine manual-review receipts across Canton, Cordera and Holly Hill require exact review; no replay was performed.

## Safeguards and gates

No financial event, invoice, refund, ledger correction, autopay opt-in, billing unpause, Stripe configuration update, schema migration, or external message was performed. Source fixes are additive; no Prisma migration is required. Roll back through protected revert of the source commit if release verification fails. Preserve existing payment and audit history. Granbury ACH, school rates, seven positive-balance-without-invoice cases, pending/returned ACH and manual-review webhooks require separate exact-target provider reconciliation before any mutation. PR #310's production schema hold remains in effect for other migrations.

Astra code review identified a potential autopay time-of-check/time-of-use race: the processor checks consent before a payment claim and does not pass the claim helper's optional topology/authorize callbacks. This needs a synthetic race test and separately coordinated fix; there is no observed production race or authorized charge test.

Separate pre-existing concurrency follow-up: the multi-family invoice route can acquire a later family's account lock after holding a Center write lock, while the recurring cron locks account before Center. This creates a potential cross-route deadlock under concurrent batches. The new synthetic test exercises two same-school recurring invoices through the real invoice helper and confirms that path avoids the prior shared-lock upgrade; it does not prove PostgreSQL cross-route batch behavior. Reconcile lock order or add transaction retry in a separate scoped change.
