# The BEE Suite School Transition Announcement Email

**Updated:** August 11, 2026
**Audience:** school owners and directors  
**Use:** standalone owner/director announcement sent manually before the new week. This is not an automated or dashboard-linked email.

## Ready-To-Send Email

**Subject:** The BEE Suite Is Ready - Your School Can Transition When You're Ready 🐝

Hi Owners and Directors,

We're excited to announce that The BEE Suite is live and ready for every school to begin using when its team is ready to transition from ProCare.

For schools that submitted ProCare exports this past week:

- Your families' and children's information has been imported into The BEE Suite and is ready for director review.
- Parent Portal invitations have been emailed to the approved guardian addresses associated with each family's account, including the login and browser-install instructions needed to get started.
- Owners were emailed a secure Stripe onboarding link last week to connect and verify the school's payout bank account.

If an owner needs the Stripe link resent, a parent did not receive the invitation, or an imported record needs to be corrected, please reply to this email and let us know.

To become fully operational, each school needs:

1. The director's completed ProCare exports and review of the imported records.
2. The owner's completed payout-bank setup and verification through the secure Stripe link.
3. Confirmation that director, teacher, billing, and parent access is correct for the school.
4. A coordinated transition date that identifies the last tuition cycle handled in ProCare and the first tuition cycle handled in The BEE Suite.

Please do not invoice, collect, or process the same tuition cycle in both systems. Before discontinuing billing through ProCare, we will help the school confirm its first BEE Suite billing date, opening balances, credits, tuition assignments, payment methods, payout readiness, and reconciliation owner. ProCare should remain the billing source of record until that written cutover is approved.

The BEE Suite supports the core workflows our schools use every day, including family and child records, billing and payments, attendance, check-in and check-out, staff, classrooms, communication, documents, reporting, enrollment, CRM, and parent and teacher portals. It was designed specifically for our schools to provide a more connected experience, stronger operational flexibility, and lower software costs.

There is no need to make an unplanned immediate switch. Schools may transition when ready, and support will be available throughout setup, validation, launch, and ongoing use. Before the first live payment cycle, each school should confirm its family records, balances, enrollment, staff access, payout account, billing schedule, and support plan.

The attached launch packet includes:

- Owner payout setup and verification
- Director setup, family and child validation, and launch sign-off
- Billing and payment cutover controls
- Parent Portal login and browser-install instructions
- Teacher Portal and attendance procedures
- Kiosk check-in and check-out procedures
- Support and troubleshooting guidance

We're incredibly excited for everyone to begin using The BEE Suite and to bring school management, family communication, enrollment, and payments into one better platform.

If you need help getting started or would like us to review your school's readiness before launch, please reach out. We're here to make the transition as smooth as possible.

Let's get ready to BEE even better!

Best,  
Brenden Bruner  
The BEE Suite | Kid City USA Enterprises

## Sender Check Before Sending

- Confirm the final owner/director recipient list.
- Confirm the submitted-school import, parent-invitation, and Stripe-link statements above are accurate for the recipients.
- Do not attach ProCare exports, bank information, passwords, payment details, medical files, or custody files.
- Attach the current packet from `output/pdf/SCHOOL_TRANSITION_EMAIL_PACKET_CURRENT/`.
- Send this message manually; do not add it to the dashboard-linked email sequence.
- Keep the email informational. Sending it does not activate billing, payments, kiosk use, new invitations, or ProCare retirement.
