# Review enrollment and export the roster

Source-reviewed step-by-step lesson. Instructional slides, not a recording of a completed live action.

## Step 1

Confirm the school in the workspace header.

## Step 2

From Dashboard, select View enrollment status, or open Insights & Reputation → Enrollment status.

## Step 3

Review current totals grouped by classroom and age; search for the intended child or classroom.

## Step 4

Resolve unexpected classroom or enrollment-status discrepancies in the underlying record using verified school information.

## Step 5

Use the available CSV, PDF or print action for the reviewed view.

## Step 6

Verify the exported school, scope and totals before storing or sharing it. Use Show Past & Other in the family workspace when reviewing historical records.

## Verify the result

Export totals and active roster agree with the selected school.
