# App Review safety changes — September 29, 2026

Parent and Teacher received messages provide **Report** and **Block sender**. Blocking is a private recipient preference: it hides that sender's messages in the recipient's inbox and history, prevents direct replies to a blocked recipient, and suppresses email, SMS and push message copies. Pending deliveries recheck the current preference. Assigned messages and unassigned parent replies check the recipient again inside the message transaction after uploads. Notification insertion takes the recipient row lock also used by blocking: notifications committed first are archived by a later block, and notifications attempted after a block are skipped. **Blocked senders > Unblock** restores visibility and permits future copies. Incoming Twilio SMS uses the same recipient lock and sender-prefixed notification metadata. Blocking also archives that recipient’s older incoming-SMS previews without sender metadata; their original school messages remain available under the normal message scope. Other recipients and school records are preserved. Urgent safety issues should be raised directly with the school. Reporting still creates the existing authorized moderation/audit record.

Parent's **Family > Profile & Security > Privacy & account deletion** guidance gives a 30-day target, explains required school record retention and promises an emailed completion confirmation. Existing owner approval and retention review remain mandatory. Execution removes the authentication identity, revokes access/sessions, unlinks the guardian login, anonymizes the account profile and removes current and replaced personal avatars from that account's storage prefix. The completion notice is queued in the successful cleanup transaction, with retries through the existing integration-delivery worker. A failure remains pending and must not be described as completed. Operators must monitor failed completion notices and communicate revised timing when the target cannot be met.

Verification uses isolated synthetic review accounts and mocked destructive/provider paths. It must not send real school messages, execute tuition payments or delete real accounts. The shared App Review identities remain protected from deletion. Focused checks cover tenant/family/classroom isolation, forged block targets, reversible preferences, blocked pagination/replies, delivery suppression, idempotent completion, failed cleanup, late recipient blocks at message/notification commit and a photo upload finishing during deletion.

These are hosted portal/backend changes. Native build-5 archive inputs, dependency lockfiles, database schema and migrations are unchanged. The native shell loads the current HTTPS portal; the portal's Git tree is newer than the archived source. Exact processed TestFlight build-5 installations still require physical authenticated QA, light/dark screenshots and walkthrough recordings before the final Apple replies and resubmission. Local/source verification does not establish Apple approval or a public download listing.

References: [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) and [Apple account deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/).
