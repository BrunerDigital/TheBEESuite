## A consistent walkthrough

1. Explain the outcome in one sentence.
2. Name the school, role, exact record and date or service period.
3. Read the relevant guide and identify any required approval.
4. Demonstrate the task using an approved training environment, or verbally walk through the action when safe demo coverage is unavailable.
5. Describe the successful result and where it is verified.
6. Ask the trainee what they would do after a timeout, wrong record or unexpected balance.

Training completion confirms observed understanding. Live school activation, access grants, billing, invitations and other operational approvals are recorded separately.
