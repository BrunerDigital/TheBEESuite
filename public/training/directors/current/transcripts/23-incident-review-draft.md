# Prepare an incident review

Interactive synthetic form walkthrough. Demonstrates preparation and review; no save, send, access change or payment is submitted.

Source: real components from c62c6ec1, synthetic fixtures. Computer-generated narration with captions; timing follows the spoken steps. No completed server action is represented.

## 1. Confirm the exact school and record

This uses the real app editor with synthetic training records. Check the school, family or employee before changing a field.

## 2. Choose the supported review status

After checking the incident facts and response, choose the status that accurately reflects the review.

## 3. Record the follow-up task

Write a specific next step and owner. This field does not replace the school’s urgent incident process.

## 4. Keep notification and acknowledgement separate

Parent notified records an action that already happened. The parent acknowledgement timestamp is a separate state.

## 5. Review before saving

After an authorized save, reopen the incident and verify its review, notification and acknowledgement states. This review is not submitted.
