# Review the teacher workspace

Teacher-screen orientation for directors overseeing classroom work.

Narrated, captioned screen orientation. Synthetic preview; no live action is submitted. Timing follows the narrated steps.

## 1. Confirm teacher and classroom

The teacher must use their own account and assigned classroom. Check the roster before recording an event.

## 2. Review the roster

Match the selected child with the actual classroom record. Directors resolve incorrect assignments before teachers continue.

## 3. Check attendance and reports

Attendance, location, photos, daily reports and incidents have separate actions. Check the child and date for each task.

## 4. Verify sharing and sync

A saved draft is not proof of parent sharing. Review the result and confirm offline actions have synced before closing the day.
