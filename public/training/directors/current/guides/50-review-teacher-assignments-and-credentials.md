# Review teacher assignments and credentials

Category: Kiosk and staffing

Before you start: confirm the correct school and role. Use approved source records; perform live changes only within your assigned authority.

1. Open **Staff & Access → Teachers** and choose the exact teacher.
2. Confirm school, active status, title, classroom and contact details.
3. Review classroom coverage. If an assignment change is authorized, choose the intended assignment and use **Save assignment**.
4. Review certifications against source documents, including certification name, status and expiration.
5. Reopen the teacher’s record and verify the saved assignment and credential dates.
6. Confirm the teacher sees the correct roster during the school’s approved access check.

## Verify the result

Staff profile, assigned classroom and source credentials agree.

## If something goes wrong

Role grants, login provisioning, kiosk codes and deactivation need their own authorization. Wait for pending actions; reopen the record before retrying an uncertain save or send.
