# Review an employee time card

Interactive synthetic form walkthrough. Demonstrates preparation and review; no save, send, access change or payment is submitted.

Source: real components from c62c6ec1, synthetic fixtures. Computer-generated narration with captions; timing follows the spoken steps. No completed server action is represented.

## 1. Confirm the exact school and record

This uses the real app editor with synthetic training records. Check the school, family or employee before changing a field.

## 2. Open the staff time clock

Select the correct employee before reviewing punches. Do not use Clock in to repair an unknown historical event.

## 3. Select the school-local pay period

Confirm the date range used by both the punch table and payroll time cards.

## 4. Compare saved punches with shift evidence

Review clock-in, clock-out, paycode and notes. Correct only verified facts; never invent missing hours.

## 5. Review before saving

Open Payroll time cards to review decimal-hour totals. Printing and sending a summary are separate actions; neither processes payroll.
