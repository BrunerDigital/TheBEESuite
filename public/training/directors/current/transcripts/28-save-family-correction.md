Source: real components from c62c6ec1, synthetic fixtures. Computer-generated narration with captions; timing follows the spoken steps. Actions persist to a separate local JSON training store only. Production behavior and external delivery are not being certified.

## 1. Confirm the household

Choose the existing family. A contact correction is not a reason to create another household.

## 2. Make the verified correction

Change only the approved address. This demonstration uses a synthetic training address.

## 3. Save the correction once

Use Save family and wait for the result. Identity, access and billing are separate actions.

## 4. Reopen the record

Reload and compare the saved address with the intended correction. Do not rely only on a success message.
