## Director process

1. Assign the school's director as owner and record a target completion date.
2. Confirm existing authorized access, school identity, and manual-entry versus import plan in School setup. Coordinate with any existing import before creating records.
3. Review existing families first. Set up real classrooms; enter one complete household, then continue classroom by classroom.
4. Finish guardians, children, emergency contacts, pickups, safety information, schedules, and documents. Resolve missing source facts with the family.
5. Compare expected current family and child totals with entered totals. Track missing items, owner, and next follow-up. Counts alone do not establish completeness.
6. Complete director data review. Invitations, billing, payments, messaging, and cutover remain separate decisions.

Director guide: https://thebeesuite.io/resources/director-data-clean-start#family-entry
