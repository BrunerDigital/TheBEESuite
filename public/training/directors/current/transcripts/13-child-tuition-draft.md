This uses the real app editor with synthetic training records. Check the school, family or employee before changing a field.

## 2. Review the selected child and rate

## 3. Check the cadence and first service week

Review the approved billing cycle and the first service period. Check for an existing invoice before creating anything new.

This synthetic example adds a $25 weekly family discount. Review the net invoice amount; a one-week exception belongs in One-time fee / credit.
