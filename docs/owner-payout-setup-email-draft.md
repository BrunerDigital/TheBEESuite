Hi team,

Please sign in to The BEE Suite to complete your school's business verification and confirm the bank account that should receive its payouts.

**Open your owner workspace: [thebeesuite.io/owner](https://thebeesuite.io/owner)**

Use your assigned BEE Suite email and password. If this is your first sign-in, use the setup details provided securely and change your temporary password when prompted. If you already use The BEE Suite, use your current password. You can use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen if needed.

1. Confirm the selected school. If you have more than one school, choose one location at a time.
2. Select **Stripe billing and payouts** and find the school's payout setup section.
3. Choose the setup action shown for the school and follow the secure Stripe pages to complete the requested business, representative, and payout-bank information.
4. Return to The BEE Suite and check the saved status. Use **Check** in the school payout table, or **Check saved progress** if you are on the replacement-account setup page. Confirm that the default payout bank's name and last four digits match the school.
5. If you manage several schools, use **Change school** in your owner workspace and repeat for each location.

Please have the school's legal business and tax information, authorized representative details, and exact payout-bank routing and account numbers available. Stripe may request additional verification documents.

You start the process in The BEE Suite and complete the sensitive verification steps on Stripe's secure hosted pages. Your BEE Suite login and Stripe login are separate. If Stripe asks you to sign in to an existing account, use the login authorized for that account and Stripe's password-reset option if needed. Follow Stripe's instructions for access to the school's existing account; do not create another connected account.

Stripe may need time to review the information after you submit it. Completing payout setup does not confirm the school's transition date or final billing activation. Please continue the school's current approved billing process until we confirm the next step.

If your school is missing, your login does not work, or Stripe shows information you do not recognize, reply with the school name and the message you see so we can help. Please keep passwords, full bank numbers, tax identifiers, and identity documents out of email.
