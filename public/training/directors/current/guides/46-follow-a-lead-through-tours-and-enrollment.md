# Follow a lead through tours and enrollment

Category: Families and enrollment

Before you start: confirm the correct school and role. Use approved source records; perform live changes only within your assigned authority.

1. Open **Enrollment CRM → Leads** and confirm the school and exact prospective family.
2. Review existing contact information, child age/program interest and prior follow-up before adding another lead.
3. Use **Pipeline**, **Tours** or **Waitlist** for the next school-approved step; record the actual status rather than an assumed milestone.
4. When an enrollment packet is needed, select the lead and use **Send registration form**. Review the recipient before sending.
5. Once the saved family, guardian and child are verified, use the separate parent invitation workflow.
6. Confirm the next follow-up owner and date; verify enrollment and classroom state in the child record.

## Verify the result

Lead follow-up, registration, enrollment and parent access have distinct verified states.

## If something goes wrong

Do not send another registration or create a duplicate family because a prior result is unclear. Wait for pending actions; reopen the record before retrying an uncertain save or send.
