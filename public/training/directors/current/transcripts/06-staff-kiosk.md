# Orient staff to the time clock

Staff kiosk entry screen; no real employee code or punch is submitted.

Narrated, captioned screen orientation. Synthetic preview; no live action is submitted. Timing follows the narrated steps.

## 1. Use the correct school kiosk

Choose Staff and use your own staff code. Enter the work email if requested to identify the correct employee.

## 2. Check the employee identity

Before a real punch, verify the returned name, classroom and current clock status. Never use another employee’s code.

## 3. Clock once and verify

Choose Clock In or Clock Out as appropriate, then wait for the confirmed status before leaving the kiosk.

## 4. Resolve a missing punch

Directors compare the time card with verified shift evidence. Use the time-card tutorial for an approved correction.
