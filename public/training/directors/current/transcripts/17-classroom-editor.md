# Review and prepare classroom settings

Interactive synthetic form walkthrough. Demonstrates preparation and review; no save, send, access change or payment is submitted.

Source: real components from c62c6ec1, synthetic fixtures. Computer-generated narration with captions; timing follows the spoken steps. No completed server action is represented.

## 1. Confirm the exact school and record

This uses the real app editor with synthetic training records. Check the school, family or employee before changing a field.

## 2. Open the existing classroom

Choose the existing room when editing it. Review actual enrollment, assigned staff and the ratio status.

## 3. Review school, room and age group

Confirm the selected school and room name. Classroom changes affect other role views.

## 4. Review licensed capacity and ratio

Use approved school records for both values. An edited ratio does not establish real staffing compliance.

## 5. Review before saving

In normal use, save once only with authorization, wait for confirmation, then reopen the record. This demonstration stops before saving.
